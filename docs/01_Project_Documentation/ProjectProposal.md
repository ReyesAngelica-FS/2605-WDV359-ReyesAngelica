# Ala Rey Studios

## Application Definition Statement

Ala Rey Studios is a modern portfolio-style web application designed to showcase creative event planning, themed celebrations, and personalized party experiences through a visually engaging and elegant interface. Inspired by the layout and presentation style of photography portfolios, the application highlights curated event themes, décor concepts, services, and creative inspiration for clients seeking memorable celebrations.

The purpose of Ala Rey Studios is to provide users with an organized and immersive experience where they can browse event styles, explore planning ideas, and connect with a personal event planner through a clean and responsive design. The project blends creativity, branding, and modern web development practices to create a professional online presence for a growing event-planning brand.

---

# Target Market

## Primary Audience

Based on initial research into modern event-planning websites, social media trends, and lifestyle branding:

- Age Range: 21–45
- Gender: All
- Audience Type: Individuals planning birthdays, weddings, baby showers, celebrations, and social events
- Income Level: Middle-income to higher-income households
- Digital Comfort: Comfortable using modern web and mobile applications
- Interests: Event planning, décor inspiration, luxury aesthetics, themed experiences, photography-style portfolios
- Platforms Used: Pinterest, Instagram, TikTok, Etsy, Canva, lifestyle blogs

---

## Primary Research

I reviewed event-planning content across Pinterest, Instagram, and portfolio websites to better understand how users engage with visual inspiration and creative services.

### Key Insights:
- Users prefer visually organized layouts with large imagery and minimal clutter.
- Clients are drawn to brands that feel personal, modern, and aesthetically polished.
- Many users browse event inspiration before directly contacting a planner.
- Portfolio presentation and branding strongly influence credibility and trust.

---

## Secondary Research

- Visual-first branding continues to dominate creative industries such as photography, lifestyle branding, and event planning.
- Social-media-driven businesses benefit from clean portfolio websites that centralize services, galleries, and booking information.
- Responsive and mobile-friendly portfolio experiences are important because many users browse inspiration on mobile devices.

# Problem Statement

Many small event-planning businesses rely heavily on social media platforms to showcase their work, but lack a centralized and professional portfolio experience that highlights their creativity, branding, and services in an organized way.

Existing portfolio websites can often feel outdated, overly cluttered, or disconnected from the visual experience users expect from modern creative brands.

Ala Rey Studios addresses this problem by creating a clean, elegant, and responsive platform that showcases event creativity while improving user engagement and brand presentation.

---

# Pain Points

- Social media alone can feel disorganized for showcasing services
- Many portfolio websites lack modern visual presentation
- Users want inspiration quickly without navigating cluttered layouts
- Small creative brands need stronger online branding
- Potential clients want easier ways to browse and inquire about services
